Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Simply visit our website and click "Start Your Recovery Today". Fill out the intake form with your case details, and our team will contact you within 24 hours.

How do I report scammers?

You can report scammers through our detailed intake form or by contacting us directly. We'll guide you through the process and gather all necessary information.

What is the procedure for making a report?

Contact us with as much detail as possible about the scam, including transaction records, communications, and personal information. Our experts will take it from there.

How long does recovery take?

Recovery timelines vary depending on the complexity of your case. We'll provide you with an estimated timeline during your initial assessment.
